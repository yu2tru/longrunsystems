Finding leads eats your day.

  • Searching by hand is slow.

    Looking up businesses one at a time and copying names and numbers into a spreadsheet takes hours you could spend on real work.

  • Not every lead is worth a call.

    Without a way to sort them, a business that already has everything gets the same time as one that needs what you sell.

  • Leads get lost.

    Notes in your phone and half-finished spreadsheets make it easy to forget who you called and who to try again.

A lead list without the legwork.

  • You say who.

    Describe the kind of business and the area, like roofers in Venice, FL. That is the whole search.

  • It finds them.

    Matching local businesses come back with name, phone, address, rating, review count, and website.

  • It flags the best ones.

    Businesses with no website or a low review count are marked as high-potential, so you know where to start.

  • You track every lead.

    Mark where each lead stands as you work through the list, so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • It exports to CSV.

    Download any list and open it in Excel or the tools you already use.

  • Dashboard or Telegram.

    Use the simple dashboard, or message the Telegram bot and get leads back in the chat.
